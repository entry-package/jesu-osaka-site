5th Hannan University Esports Tournament

This is Hannan University's annual esports tournament, featuring Street Fighter 6. The main event was scheduled for Sunday, October 4, 2026, from 10:00 to 18:00 at the 50th Anniversary Hall on the main campus of Hannan University (5-4-33 Amami Higashi, Matsubara, Osaka Prefecture). Qualifiers were scheduled for Saturday, October 3. The tournament had 2 divisions: a university-affiliated division (each team needed 1 Hannan University student, employee, family member, friend, or other affiliated person) and a Matsubara City-sponsored open division. Entry was free and there was no participation limit. Spectators could attend freely on the day. Invited professional player Dogura was in attendance. Exhibitors included the Osaka Prefectural Police Headquarters' public safety division and the Osaka Prefecture acupuncture and massage practitioners' association. In the university-affiliated division, first prize was an Amazon Echo Dot Max and second prize was a Quntis monitor light. In the open division, first prize was an Amazon Echo Dot Max, and Matsubara City provided Makki merchandise for both first and second place. The first registration deadline for players was Thursday, September 3, 2026. Organized by Hannan University's Faculty of Informatics and Faculty of Management Information. Sponsored by Matsubara City, with official endorsement from the Hannan University supporters' association.
