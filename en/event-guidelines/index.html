Events in Osaka Prefecture

LISTING GUIDELINES

Event Listing Guidelines

Official events in Osaka with reliable information.

We will introduce major events in games and esports held locally within the Osaka Prefecture. Based on the official announcement of the organizer, we will confirm the date of the event, venue, and participation methods and publish them.

Events to be published

  • Tournaments organized or officially endorsed by JESU or its regional branches, official study sessions, and similar events
  • Official major tournaments and official game festivals organized by game developers
  • Large game event hosted by a professional team, the streamer themselves, or their managing company.
  • Major esports tournaments hosted and supported by local governments

What counts as a major event

We prioritize national and international finals, official regional qualifiers, official festivals run by game publishers, and major tournaments supported by local governments. For gaming events organized by professional teams or streamers, we look for publicly announced capacities of at least 300 people and venues such as halls or arenas. Official JESU and regional branch events are eligible regardless of size.

We do not estimate an event's size from unannounced attendance figures or follower counts alone.

PACkage-related events are not limited by scale.

We list events in Osaka Prefecture of any size or genre when official sources confirm that PACkage organizes, co-organizes, plans, operates, supports or appears at them. This includes related programs such as PACFES, PACLAN, Game Pavilion JP and OsakaPNG, as well as small networking events, study sessions and hands-on sessions.

We will provide information with the organizer's name clearly stated. We will not indicate that all events of related brands are organized by PACkage alone. We will post only those events where the date of the event, the venue within the prefecture, and the officially published information have been confirmed.

Individually reviewed listing

We listed Joshin Kishiwada GP (November 23, 2026) after checking the event summary provided to us and OeGG’s public announcement. The listing does not mean JeSU Osaka organizes or endorses the event.

We check official announcements

We check official websites run by organizers, game publishers, JESU and its regional branches, or local governments, as well as event and ticket pages they link to. For social media, we verify the link from the official site and who posted the announcement. Aggregators and fan posts alone are insufficient for a listing.

Things that are not eligible

  • Events held outside Osaka Prefecture, online exclusive events
  • Events without an announced date or venue, rumors, and unofficial information.
  • Regular store competitions, small meetups (except PACkage-related and official JESU or branch events), and regular livestreams.
  • Excluding PACkage-related items, exhibitions, videos, rides, merchandise, and performances that are not primarily intended for competition, viewing, or game play.
  • Tournaments whose organizer cannot be verified as official

For touring events in Kansai, we list only dates held in Osaka Prefecture. We do not list the days between separate event dates as event days.

How the Japanese calendar is updated

Every Monday we check official announcements and update listings for new events, changes, cancellations, and completed events. Unconfirmed information keeps its last verified date. Listings that have not been reconfirmed for more than 8 days are marked “Check for updates.” General listings normally cover the next 180 days. PACkage-related listings cover officially announced upcoming events.

About event listings

External events are run by their respective organizers. A listing on this calendar does not mean JeSU Osaka organizes or endorses the event or guarantees its details. Check the official event website for eligibility, registration status, changes or cancellations.

To request an event listing, please send official event information through the Event listing request form (Japanese). We review each request against these standards before listing it.

Publication standards v1.2 / 2026.10.20.
