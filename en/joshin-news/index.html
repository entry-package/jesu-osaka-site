Joshin Corporation joins as a supporting member

With social contribution and industry contribution as the core, further promotion of the esports scene in Osaka.

July 1, 2026 Japan esports Union Osaka (General Incorporated Association, JeSU Osaka)

Japan esports Union Osaka would like to inform you that Joshin Corporation has joined as a supporting member of this association.

New member company
Joshin Corporation
Membership category
a supporting member
Membership date
July 1, 2026

Japan esports Union Osaka (JeSU Osaka), based in Osaka Prefecture and represented by Masayuki Igusa, announces that Joshin Corporation, based in Naniwa Ward, Osaka, and formerly named Joshin Denki Co., Ltd., has joined as a supporting member.

Joshin operates Joshin home appliance stores and has expanded its esports activities under Joshin eSPORTS in recent years. Both organizations share a commitment to promoting esports in Osaka and have participated in the Osaka eSports Growth Guild (OeGG), which Osaka Prefecture established to bring together industry, government and academia.

With the membership of this supporting member, JeSU Osaka and Joshin will deepen their cooperation further, focusing on social contribution, industry contribution, and promoting the development of the Osaka esports scene, and will push Osaka and the Kansai region to become a representative region of Japanese esports.

Previous work and how the partnership began

Joshin has helped grow the competitive scene and fan community by organizing the professional Joshin CUP tournament and operating eSPORTS Arena KOBE Sannomiya, one of the largest permanent esports facilities in western Japan.

Under its comprehensive partnership agreement with Izumisano City, Joshin continues to support the esports Game Creator Academy, a hands-on career experience event. Through public-private collaboration, Joshin also helps develop the next generation of digital talent and revitalize the region.

On the other hand, JeSU Osaka has supported the promotion of esports in Osaka Prefecture through lectures for companies, schools, and municipalities, equipment rental and cooperation for various events, business and job matching, and the promotion of large-scale esports events domestically and internationally.

Future outlook

Through this membership, JeSU Osaka and Joshin will deepen their collaboration around the following three pillars and work together to develop esports in Osaka and the wider Kansai region.

Social contribution

We will promote regional revitalization through esports, the creation of cross-generational exchanges, and the development of next-generation digital talent.

Industry contribution

We will contribute to establishing a competitive environment and a business foundation where athletes and the community can thrive, by leveraging our know-how in event management and facilities and equipment.

Promotion of esports in Osaka

Through the promotion of domestic and international events and strengthening collaboration between industry, government, and academia, we will disseminate the esports culture originating in Osaka to both domestic and international audiences.

About Joshin Corporation

Joshin Corporation (formerly Joshin Denki Co., Ltd., renamed on April 1, 2026) operates the Joshin chain of home appliance stores. As Joshin eSPORTS, the company organizes the Joshin CUP tournament, sponsors professional teams, operates the permanent eSPORTS Arena KOBE Sannomiya, and supports esports in partnership with schools and local governments.

About Japan esports Union Osaka (JeSU Osaka)

JeSU Osaka (Japan esports Union Osaka, a general incorporated association) is an organization that promotes "gamer first/game community first" and works to develop and promote the esports industry in Osaka Prefecture. We conduct a wide range of activities, including lectures for companies, schools, and municipalities, equipment rental and cooperation for various events, business and job matching, participation in Osaka Prefecture's "Osaka eSports Growth Guild (OeGG)," promotion of large-scale esports events domestically and internationally, publication of member newsletters and study sessions, and so on.

Representative Director: Masayuki Igusa
