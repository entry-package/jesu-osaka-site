Free registration is open,
and our official community has launched

For players, teams, referees, coaches, commentators, streamers, and production and streaming staff,
and for companies and organizations involved in esports.

Japan esports Union Osaka (JeSU Osaka) opened free registration on Tuesday, November 17, 2026. It is open to players, teams, referees, coaches, commentators, streamers, and production and streaming staff, including freelancers, who are active in Osaka, as well as esports-related companies, local governments, schools, venues, media outlets, and other organizations. We also launched the official JeSU Osaka Discord community, which anyone aged 13 or older can join.

JeSU Osaka maintains this free directory independently. Registration is separate from licensing, official certification, and membership with the national body, Japan Esports Union (JESU). It does not grant any qualification or official recognition.

Individual and team registration

If you opt in, we’ll send you news about competitions and events and requests for appearances, speaking, or interviews. Registration is free, and you can cancel at any time using the Contact form. Registration takes about 5 minutes.

  • Who can register: players, referees and tournament operations staff, instructors and coaches, school club advisers, commentators and MCs, streamers and video creators, production and streaming staff (including video, audio, lighting, camera, editing and design), and event staff (including check-in and setup). A team representative can register the team.
  • Freelance production and streaming staff are welcome to register. If you opt in, we will pass on staffing requests for tournaments, events, and streams.
  • If you are under 16, fill out the form with your parent or guardian. Their consent is required.
  • If a team representative is under 16, a parent or guardian must consent, fill out the form with them and provide their own contact information. If the representative is 16 or 17, obtain permission from a teacher who advises the team or from a parent or guardian, then enter that adult’s contact information. Ask each person for permission before entering their details, including team members.
  • For requests involving anyone under 18, we proceed only after a parent or guardian gives consent. For those aged 16 or 17, we ask them for their parent or guardian's contact details each time.
  • JeSU Osaka only passes on requests and, with your consent, puts you in touch with the requester. We do not arrange employment or freelance work. You and the requester agree on pay, terms, and contracts directly. Registration does not guarantee a request or work.
  • Your real name, contact information, and date of birth are not published in the online directory. The directory lists only the display name, role, game titles, and municipality of people who opt in. You can change your preference later.

Company and organization registration

Registration is open to companies, local governments, schools, and organizations that work in or are interested in esports in Osaka. We use the directory for discussions about cooperation, introductions to players and teams, information on competitions and seminars, and invitations to take part in research. Registration is free, and you can cancel at any time using the Contact form. Registration takes about 3 minutes.

If you request an invitation when registering, the JeSU Osaka team will invite you to our private Discord lounge for companies and organizations. It is separate from our public community. We do not share registrants’ personal information in the lounge.

Official community (Discord)

This is a place where you can get information about competitions and events, recruit teams and members, and chat about esports. This is a community for people who enjoy and support esports in Osaka.

  • Only people 13 years old and older can participate (according to the Discord terms of use). For those under 16 years of age, please read the rules with your guardian before participating.
  • Do not post real names, schools, addresses, phone numbers, or contact details such as LINE IDs. This applies to your information and anyone else’s.
  • Participants aged 18 or over, including organizers, coaches, referees, and company representatives, must not send direct messages to or have one-to-one calls with anyone under 18. Communicate in a public channel or a setting with at least 2 adults present. When you do not know the other person's age, use a public channel instead of direct messages.
  • If another participant sends you a DM or invites you to another app, do not respond. Report it to us. You can turn off direct messages from members of this server in Discord’s settings.
  • Our staff will never ask for passwords, money, or personal information by DM. Be careful of any DM claiming to be from our staff or JeSU Osaka.
  • The official community is operated by JeSU Osaka. Please contact JeSU Osaka for inquiries.

When you're in trouble or scared (please read this with your parents or teachers as well)

Our automatic filters help block posts sharing contact details, inviting others to DMs or other apps, or soliciting money. The filters cannot catch everything. Our moderators cannot see DMs between members. Moderators monitor the server and may remove posts, warn members, or remove members who break the rules.

If you see a rule violation or anything that makes you feel scared or uncomfortable, use the Report or ask for help (form) (Japanese) to let us know. You can send a report without giving your name or contact details. We will not tell the person reported or other participants who made the report. We may share information with the police or other authorities when someone's life or safety is at risk or when the law requires it. If someone's life is in danger or you need urgent help, also call the police at 110. If you are under 18, you can also call the 24-hour Child SOS line at 0120-0-78310.

Only JeSU Osaka staff can use the information submitted through the reporting and help form. They use it only to respond to reports and protect participants' safety.

Handling the information entrusted to us

The information you provide in the registration form will be handled as follows (the same content as the last page of each form).

  1. Data controller: Japan esports Union Osaka (General Incorporated Association, known as JeSU Osaka). Representative Director: Masayuki Igusa. Address: 1-15-17 Toyoshima-minami, Ikeda, Osaka 563-0035, Japan. We entrust intake, directory management and communications to our secretariat, PACkage Inc. PACkage uses the information only for the work we assign and does not use it for its own sales or marketing.
  2. How we use your information: (1) To manage the register and contact you about registration. (2) To share information and requests about competitions, events, appearances, speaking, media interviews, and cooperation only if you choose to receive them. (3) For statistics and research. JeSU Osaka may publish aggregated results in its reports, with no individual identifiable. We leave out small groups if anyone might be identifiable. (4) To share announcements from JeSU Osaka. (5) To list the information you choose to publish on the JeSU Osaka website.
  3. Sharing with other people or companies: We do not share your information without your consent. Before passing your contact details to a client, such as a company, we ask for your permission each time. If you are under 16, we ask your parent or guardian. The same applies when PACkage Inc., our secretariat, is the client. Exceptions apply when required by law or when we must protect someone’s life, physical safety or property and obtaining your consent is difficult.
  4. Online directory: We list only the information you agree to publish. We never list your real name, contact information, or date of birth.
  5. Storage: We will accept and store it on Google LLC (United States) services (Google Forms and Google Drive). Only the secretariat and board members can view it (board members can only view it within the scope of their duties).
  6. Retention: We retain your registration until you ask to cancel it or for up to 2 years from your last update. When it reaches 2 years, we will contact you before deleting it.
  7. Access, correction and cancellation: To check or correct your information, ask us to stop using it, or have it deleted, contact us using the details below.
  8. If you are under 16, you need consent from a parent or guardian.
  9. Contact: JeSU Osaka Contact form
